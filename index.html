Canvas of Kindness Foundation

Canvas of Kindness Foundation is a student-led nonprofit dedicated to spreading kindness through handmade cards and artwork for hospitals, senior centers, and communities in need. Volunteers can earn verified service hours while making a positive impact. We're currently recruiting graphic designers and chapter founders worldwide.
